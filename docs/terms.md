## What Squared Away does

Squared Away connects to your business Gmail. It sorts incoming email, writes reply drafts in your
voice, puts them in your queue and in your Gmail Drafts folder, and drafts follow-ups on quotes and
invoices that have gone quiet (at most two per conversation). How we handle your information is
explained in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

only within the limits described in our Privacy Policy; it waits 10 minutes before each send so
you can stop it.
