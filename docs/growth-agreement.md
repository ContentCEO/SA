## 2. What we do for you

We do the work ticked below, one-on-one. The list matches the services on our Growth page.

- [ ] **Google ads** — set up and run ads that can show when someone nearby searches for your
      work; watch the spend; report in plain words.
- [ ] **Facebook ads** — ads shown to people in the towns you work in, built from your own photos.
- [ ] **Instagram ads** — your finished work, shown to people in the towns you work in.
- [ ] **Social media posts** — regular posts of your real jobs, from photos you send us.
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — fill it in properly (services, towns, hours, photos) and keep
      it up to date.
- [ ] **Reviews** — a simple way to ask your customers for a Google review, and help answering
      the reviews you get. We never write fake reviews, buy followers or post photos of someone
      else's work.
- [ ] **Website** — a clean, fast site that works on a phone and says what you do, where you work
      and how to reach you.

Anything else is extra and only starts once we've both agreed to it in writing (email is fine).

your Growth dashboard. Growth never uses your email: if you also use the Squared Away inbox
app, nothing from it is used for Growth work, ads, posts or your Growth dashboard.
